# Business Model

> **Summary:** NEFE is designed to create measurable commercial value by helping complementary businesses coordinate acquisition, retention, and customer experiences.

## Value proposition

Individual businesses often lack the context, reach, or operating mechanism to coordinate customer journeys with complementary partners. NEFE provides a governed structure for discovering opportunities, activating approved commercial activity, and measuring results.

## Value creation

- Merchants may gain qualified reach and partner-supported journeys.
- Consumers may receive more relevant, connected experiences.
- Operators may develop stronger commercial ecosystems.
- Business leaders gain a clearer basis for investment decisions.

## Commercial model

Commercial terms can vary by deployment. Possible arrangements may include:

- Platform access.
- Implementation services.
- Pilot fees.
- Enterprise licensing.
- Performance-linked components.

> **Important:** Specific pricing, revenue share, token economics, or outcome guarantees are not established by this document. They require an approved commercial agreement.

## Status boundaries

The core business model is **Live** as a documented operating thesis. Individual platform modules may be **Pilot** or **Proposed** as described on their pages.
