# Enterprise Deployment

> **Summary:** Enterprise deployment requires an agreed operating model, security review, data boundaries, integration scope, support model, and measurable acceptance criteria.

## Deployment workstreams

- **Commercial:** Objectives, participant model, terms, and success measures.
- **Governance:** Ownership, approvals, escalation, and audit expectations.
- **Security:** Identity, access, environments, monitoring, and incident handling.
- **Data:** Purpose, sources, retention, residency, and deletion.
- **Integration:** Systems, exchange method, validation, and rollback.
- **Operations:** Support, training, change control, and service expectations.

## Status

**Proposed framework:** Enterprise deployment capabilities and commitments are not implied by this page. Architecture, hosting, authentication, APIs, service levels, and compliance evidence must be confirmed for the target engagement.

## Phased approach

1. Discovery and risk assessment
2. Controlled [pilot](./pilot-program.md)
3. Evidence review and gap closure
4. Limited production rollout
5. Measured expansion

## Acceptance

Acceptance criteria should cover:

- Functional journeys.
- Security controls.
- Data quality.
- Performance and resilience.
- Support.
- Commercial measurement.
