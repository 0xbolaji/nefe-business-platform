# Campaign Builder

> **Summary:** Campaign Builder supports the controlled definition of an audience, partner journey, offer, timing, approvals, and measurement plan.

## Campaign components

Every campaign should identify:

- Business objective and accountable owner
- Participating merchants and locations
- Eligible audience or context
- Offer value, restrictions, and expiry
- Customer-facing terms
- Approval status
- Baseline and success measures

## Workflow

1. Create a draft with a clear objective.
2. Select only approved participants and data.
3. Add offer terms and customer communications.
4. Review operational capacity and exception handling.
5. Obtain required approvals.
6. Test the journey.
7. Activate for the agreed period.
8. Review results in [analytics](../platform/analytics.md).

## Status

**Pilot:** Campaign configuration and approval features depend on the deployment.

**Proposed:** Autonomous campaign launch or optimization without human approval is not live.

> **Best Practice:** Use the smallest viable audience for the first launch and document every material change.
