# Verification

> **Summary:** Verification helps a network operator confirm that a participating business and its representatives meet the deployment’s eligibility requirements.

## What may be verified

Depending on jurisdiction and program policy, verification may cover business identity, registration details, authorized representatives, operating locations, contact channels, and acceptance of participation terms.

## Status

**Pilot:** Verification is an operator-controlled process. Required evidence, providers, review times, and renewal periods depend on the deployment.

## Outcomes

- **Pending:** Information is under review or incomplete.
- **Verified:** Current requirements have been satisfied.
- **Action required:** Corrections or additional evidence are needed.
- **Suspended or declined:** Participation is restricted under the applicable policy.

Verification is not an endorsement of a merchant’s products, financial condition, or future conduct.

## Data handling

Collect only the evidence required for the stated purpose. Restrict access, document retention, and avoid placing sensitive verification material in campaign content or general profile fields. See [privacy and data protection](../security/privacy-data-protection.md).

## After verification

Complete [merchant onboarding](./onboarding.md), assign [roles and permissions](../security/roles-permissions.md), and establish a review date.
