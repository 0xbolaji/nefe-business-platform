# Founder’s Collection

> **Summary:** Founder’s Collection is a proposed recognition and participation concept; it is not documented as a live financial product or transferable asset.

## Intended purpose

The concept may provide a structured way to recognize early contributors, record participation milestones, or create access to approved founder-community experiences.

## Status

**Proposed:** Eligibility, benefits, issuance, transferability, technology, and legal form have not been established by this documentation.

## Design principles

- Benefits must be specific, supportable, and approved.
- Recognition should not imply ownership, equity, profit rights, or guaranteed access.
- Personal information and participation history require appropriate controls.
- Any scarcity or tiering should support a real community purpose.
- Public claims require legal, commercial, and brand review.

## Relationship to NEFE

The core NEFE platform and its [business model](../business/business-model.md) should remain understandable without this concept. Any token-related proposal is separately governed under [token utility](./token-utility.md).
