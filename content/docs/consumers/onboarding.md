# Consumer Onboarding

> **Summary:** Consumer onboarding should explain the experience, request only necessary information, and provide meaningful choice before participation.

## Experience principles

1. State who operates the experience.
2. Explain the immediate customer value.
3. Present material terms before commitment.
4. Request only information needed for the selected journey.
5. Provide accessible consent and preference controls where required.
6. Explain how to obtain support or exercise privacy rights.

## Status

**Pilot:** Consumer onboarding flows vary by deployment, merchant, channel, and jurisdiction. A documented concept does not imply availability in every environment.

## Trust and transparency

Customers should be able to distinguish merchant communications from network-level communications. Rewards, eligibility, expiry, and redemption conditions should be clear and discoverable.

> **Important:** Participation must not depend on hidden data sharing or misleading consent.
