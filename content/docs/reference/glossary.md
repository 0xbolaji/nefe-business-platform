# Glossary

> **Summary:** Use these definitions consistently in product, commercial, and technical discussions.

## Terms

**Business-first**  
An approach that begins with a commercial problem, accountable owner, and measurable outcome before selecting technology.

**Campaign**  
A bounded commercial activity with defined participants, audience, terms, duration, approvals, and measures.

**Commerce Graph**  
NEFE’s governed conceptual model of participating businesses, relationships, journeys, and permitted signals.

**Consumer**  
A customer or prospective customer interacting with a NEFE-enabled experience.

**Live**  
Available in the current approved product environment.

**Merchant**  
A participating business that offers products, services, experiences, or customer value.

**Network operator**  
The organization or accountable team governing a NEFE deployment and its participants.

**Opportunity**  
A decision-support candidate for a potential commercial relationship, journey, or campaign; not a guaranteed outcome.

**Pilot**  
Available only within a controlled evaluation or configured deployment.

**Proposed**  
A direction or concept that is not implemented or contractually committed.

**Referral**  
A defined customer progression between participating businesses or experiences.
