# Frequently Asked Questions

> **Summary:** These answers clarify NEFE’s purpose, participation model, and current capability boundaries.

## What is NEFE?

NEFE Business Network is a business-first commercial platform for coordinating approved customer value, referrals, partner activity, and performance insight. See the [introduction](../getting-started/index.md).

## Is every documented capability live?

No. Pages distinguish **Live**, **Pilot**, and **Proposed** capabilities. Definitions are in the [documentation system](../DOCUMENTATION-SYSTEM.md#capability-labels).

## Does NEFE replace merchant systems?

No. NEFE is intended as a coordination and insight layer. Integration scope is deployment-specific; see [integration concepts](../developers/integration-concepts.md).

## Are public APIs available?

This documentation does not claim an implemented public API. See [API readiness](../developers/api-readiness.md).

## Does NEFE guarantee revenue?

No. Analytics and opportunities support commercial decisions but cannot guarantee outcomes. See [analytics](../platform/analytics.md).

## Is a token required?

No. Token utility is a [proposed concept](../ecosystem/token-utility.md), separate from the core platform.

## How does a business start?

Follow the [quick start](../getting-started/quick-start.md) and prepare a controlled [pilot program](../business/pilot-program.md).

## How is data protected?

Review the [security overview](../security/overview.md) and [privacy guidance](../security/privacy-data-protection.md). Exact controls must be confirmed per deployment.
