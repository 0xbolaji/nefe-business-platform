# Quick Start

> **Summary:** Define the commercial objective, select a bounded participant group, agree measurement and governance, and then configure a controlled pilot.

## 1. Define the outcome

Choose one primary business outcome, such as qualified referrals, cross-merchant conversion, customer retention, or campaign efficiency. Record:

- The baseline.
- The target.
- The measurement period.
- The accountable owner.

## 2. Select the network

Identify a small group of complementary businesses with a credible shared customer journey. Avoid combining direct competitors unless the governance model explicitly supports it.

## 3. Confirm readiness

Review:

- [Merchant onboarding](../merchants/onboarding.md)
- [Privacy and data protection](../security/privacy-data-protection.md)
- [Roles and permissions](../security/roles-permissions.md)
- [Integration concepts](../developers/integration-concepts.md)

> **Important:** NEFE documentation does not imply the availability of public production APIs. Confirm integration options for the specific deployment before committing technical resources.

## 4. Design the pilot

Define:

- Offer rules.
- Participating locations.
- Customer eligibility.
- The approval process.
- The support model.
- The reporting cadence.

Use the [pilot program](../business/pilot-program.md) as the operating guide.

## 5. Configure and verify

- Complete participant verification.
- Assign least-privilege roles.
- Review campaign content.
- Test the intended journey using approved non-production data.

## 6. Launch and learn

Begin with a controlled audience. Monitor:

- Customer experience.
- Operational exceptions.
- Partner contribution.
- Commercial performance.

Change one material variable at a time so results remain interpretable.

## Exit criteria

A pilot should conclude with a documented scale, revise, or stop decision. See [analytics](../platform/analytics.md) and [enterprise deployment](../business/enterprise-deployment.md).
