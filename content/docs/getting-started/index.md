# Introduction

> **Summary:** NEFE Business Network helps complementary businesses coordinate customer value, referrals, offers, and commercial insight through a shared operating model.

## What NEFE is

NEFE is a business-first commercial platform. It is designed to help participating businesses discover compatible partners, create measurable customer journeys, and understand how value moves across a local or sector-based network.

The platform does not replace each participant’s core systems. It provides a coordination layer for approved commercial activities and shared insight.

## Who participates

- **Merchants** configure their profile, offers, campaigns, and partner participation.
- **Consumers** discover relevant experiences and choose whether to engage.
- **Network operators** establish governance, approve participation, and monitor outcomes.
- **Business leaders** evaluate network performance and commercial return.

The website and documentation describe a combination of **Live**, **Pilot**, and **Proposed** capabilities. See the [documentation system](../DOCUMENTATION-SYSTEM.md#capability-labels) for definitions. No proposed capability should be treated as currently available.

## Principles

1. Commercial outcomes come before technology choices.
2. Participation is governed and permission-based.
3. Customer data is minimized and purpose-limited.
4. Recommendations support human decisions; they do not replace accountability.
5. Performance should be measured against an agreed baseline.
