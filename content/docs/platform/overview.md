# Platform Overview

> **Summary:** NEFE coordinates participating businesses, customer experiences, commercial opportunities, and measurement within a governed network.

## Platform layers

| Layer | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Participant operations | Profiles, onboarding, roles, and merchant workflows | Live or pilot, depending on deployment |
| Commerce Graph | Structured view of commercial relationships and activity | Pilot |
| Opportunity Engine | Decision support for identifying commercial opportunities | Pilot |
| Analytics | Outcome reporting and network performance views | Pilot |
| AI layer | Assisted analysis and explanation | Proposed unless explicitly enabled |

## Operating model

- Network operators establish participation rules.
- Merchants contribute approved business information and configure commercial activity.
- Consumers interact with experiences under disclosed terms.
- The platform records appropriate operational and outcome signals for measurement.

NEFE should not be described as an autonomous marketplace, financial institution, or universal customer-data repository.

## Key journeys

- Build a governed participant network.
- Discover complementary commercial relationships.
- Create and approve offers or campaigns.
- Measure referrals, engagement, and attributable outcomes.
- Review opportunities with accountable human decision-makers.
