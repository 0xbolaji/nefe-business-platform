# AI Layer

> **Summary:** NEFE’s AI direction is to assist authorized users with explanation, discovery, and analysis while preserving human accountability.

## Intended role

An AI-assisted layer may help:

- Summarize network context.
- Explain opportunity signals.
- Draft analysis.
- Surface questions for review.

It should operate within approved data boundaries and provide users with enough context to challenge its output.

## Current status

**Proposed unless explicitly enabled for a controlled deployment.** This documentation does not claim that a production AI service, autonomous agent, or externally accessible AI API is live.

## Guardrails

- Human approval for consequential commercial actions.
- Least-privilege access to data and tools.
- Clear indication when content is machine-assisted.
- Monitoring for quality, drift, and inappropriate output.
- No unsupported claims of certainty or guaranteed performance.
- Escalation paths for security, privacy, or business concerns.

## Appropriate use

AI output may be treated as decision support. It must not be treated as:

- Legal advice.
- A contractual commitment.
- A credit decision.
- An authoritative statement about a customer.
