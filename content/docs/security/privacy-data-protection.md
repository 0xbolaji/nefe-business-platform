# Privacy and Data Protection

> **Summary:** NEFE deployments should use only the data needed for defined commercial purposes and protect it throughout its lifecycle.

## Core principles

1. Define the purpose before collecting or receiving data.
2. Identify the responsible parties and lawful basis where required.
3. Minimize fields, precision, audience, and retention.
4. Provide appropriate notice and meaningful choices.
5. Protect data in transit, at rest, and in use according to risk.
6. Support correction, deletion, objection, or access rights as applicable.
7. Document sharing, processors, and cross-border considerations.

## Data categories

Deployments may handle:

- Business profile data.
- User account data.
- Campaign configuration.
- Operational events.
- Aggregated commercial measures.

The exact data inventory must be documented per deployment.

> **Important:** Do not place payment credentials, government identifiers, sensitive verification evidence, or unnecessary personal data into general-purpose profile, campaign, or analytics fields.

## Retention and deletion

Retention should reflect:

- Purpose.
- Contractual and legal obligations.
- Security needs.
- Customer expectations.

Pilot closeout must include an explicit retention or deletion decision.
